This product does not fully comply with Legal Metrology labeling requirements.

Violations

Violation 1

MRP Font Size Too Small

The Maximum Retail Price printed on the package is displayed in a font size smaller than what is legally required for a package of this size.

Violated Rule

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 — Rule 6, sub-rule (1)(f): Minimum font size for MRP declaration based on package area.

Violation 2

Missing Manufacturer Address

The package does not display the complete name and address of the manufacturer or packer, only a brand name is shown.

Violated Rule

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 — Rule 6, sub-rule (1)(a): Mandatory declaration of manufacturer's name and complete address.

Violation 3

Net Quantity Not in Standard Unit

Net quantity is declared as "approx. 50g" — an approximate value is not permitted; the exact net quantity in standard metric units must be stated.

Violated Rule

Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 — Rule 6, sub-rule (1)(b): Net quantity must be declared in exact standard units (grams, millilitres, etc.), not approximated.
